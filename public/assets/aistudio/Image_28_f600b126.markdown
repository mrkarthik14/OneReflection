## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Playful Editorial Minimalism** with structured **Pastel Bento UI**. Built as an Android-first experience for social productivity and self-care tracking, it merges the tactile friendliness of boutique lifestyle journals with the modular utility of modern productivity suites.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Warm & Reassuring:** A soft ivory canvas prevents the sterile, clinical feel of standard task managers, instilling calm and mindful reflection.
- **Expressive & Whimsical:** Organic sticker shapes, multi-pointed starbursts, and asymmetric bento blocks celebrate human individuality and small daily victories.
- **Crisp & Editorial:** Razor-sharp typography and high-contrast ink blacks (#171717) anchor the cheerful pastels in serious, intentional design hierarchy.

### Design Movement & Influences
- **Editorial Bento Grid:** Inspired by asymmetric magazine spreads and modular dashboard cards that group content into self-contained, color-coded candy blocks.
- **Sticker-Book Tactility:** Organic geometric badges (starbursts, scalloped blobs, tilted tags) act as micro-rewards for completed habits and scheduled milestones.
- **Grounded Contrast:** Floating pill navigation rails, dark button states, and deep black copy provide structural weight against airy pastel surfaces.

## Colors

The palette revolves around a calming **Warm Ivory** base, balanced by pitch-black contrast tokens and a 6-tone pastel spectrum assigned dynamically to categories, habits, and bento cards.

### Core Canvas & Structure
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF6EC`):** The foundational ivory surface. Used for global page backgrounds, sheet backdrops, and negative space.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for nested inputs, segmented controls, and secondary popovers.
- **Text & Contrast Ink (`#171717`):** Deep charcoal/soft black for headlines, actionable pill buttons, floating tab docks, and primary icons.
- **Muted Ink (`#6E6D68`):** Mid-tone neutral for secondary timestamps, captions, and inactive tab states.

### Pastel Bento Palette
- **Candy Pink (`#F5ACD7`):** Primary accent. Used for the prominent circular floating action button, health metrics, and urgent highlight cards.
- **Butter Yellow (`#F9DF72`):** Secondary accent. Used for active focus modules, sun/morning trackers, and grooming badges.
- **Lavender Blue (`#B7C5F3`):** Tertiary accent. Employed for mental reflection, water balance, and evening sleep logs.
- **Matcha Green (`#B8E98E`):** Semantic accent for completion statuses, physical activity, and pet medication doses.
- **Soft Cyan (`#A5E8EF`):** Semantic accent for hydration, quick notes, and environmental reminders.
- **Lilac (`#E7A2EE`):** Supplemental accent for social events, shared routines, and special milestones.

### Contrast Application Rules
- All colored cards take `#171717` text and glyphs to guarantee high legibility across all pastels.
- White text is restricted exclusively to primary dark elements: the black floating pill navigation, dark submission buttons, and dark modal overlays.

## Typography

The type scale combines the technical, geometric personality of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced humanist warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Font Pairing Logic
- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Big Metrics):** Features distinct geometric counters and editorial flair. Used for screen titles, category cards, massive stat metrics (e.g., `8.8` score or `123 bpm`), and conversational hero questions.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Micro-UI):** Provides friendly rounded terminals, tall x-height, and optimal reading performance for nested schedule lists, pet instructions, calendar grids, and interactive button chips.

### Editorial Treatments
- Metric values combine a large `display-lg` numeric string with a micro `label-md` or `label-sm` unit anchored right below or beside it.
- Title cases remain conversational and editorial (e.g., *"Taigo, how are you feeling today?"* or *"Activities just for your taste"*).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt rhythmic grid optimized for one-handed mobile ergonomic usage, supporting tablet bento expansion.

### Grid & Composition
- **Mobile Grid (Primary Form Factor):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Cards snap into asymmetric modular bento arrangements (1x1 square widgets, 2x1 full-width highlight cards, and 2x2 tall insight blocks).
- **Tablet Reflow:** Expands to an 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Bento cards maintain aspect ratios and nest side-by-side rather than stacking vertically.
- **Safe Area Insets:** Ample bottom padding (`80px` + safe-area-inset) is reserved on all scrollable screens to prevent interactive elements from clashing with the floating navigation pill.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids traditional Drop Shadow and Skewed Skeuomorphism in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Crisp Overlays**, and **Gentle Ambient Floats**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** Warm Ivory (`#FAF6EC`) global screen canvas.
2. **Bento Surface (Level 1):** Solid pastel card fills (Butter Yellow, Candy Pink, Lavender Blue, Matcha Green) resting completely flat on the canvas without drop shadows. Edge boundaries are delineated cleanly by high-contrast hue shifts.
3. **Floating Floating Actions & Overlays (Level 2):** Floating navigation bars and modal sheets lift subtly with a tailored soft ambient shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.08)`
4. **Interactive Sticker Accents (Level 3):** Organic badges, circular starbursts, and completion stamps float atop cards with slight rotation transforms (`-3deg` to `+4deg`), reinforcing an tactile paper-cut aesthetic.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy prioritizes generous, friendly curves complemented by asymmetrical bento proportions and expressive organic silhouettes.

### Corner Radius System
- **Fully Pill-Shaped (`9999px`):** Used universally for action buttons, filter chips, calendar day toggles, and the floating navigation dock.
- **Super-Rounded Bento (`24px` / `32px`):** Used for large content containers, schedule cards, and modular health trackers.
- **Mini Bento (`16px`):** Used for nested sub-cards, pill time-slots, and dosage input fields.

### Organic Stickers & Decorative Geometry
- **Starbursts & Blobs:** 8-point and 12-point softened starburst stamps indicate priority tags (`Top priority today`, `New`).
- **Scalloped Blobs & Pill Capsules:** Asymmetrical, non-standard geometric silhouettes serve as container chips for habits (e.g., cloud-shaped water tracker, wavy heart pill).

## Components

### 1. Floating Pill Navigation Bar
- **Shell:** Deep charcoal/black (`#171717`) pill centered at the bottom of the viewport with a fixed height of `64px`, featuring `9999px` corner radius.
- **Icons:** Inactive states rendered in `#FAF6EC` with `60%` opacity; active state highlighted with an ivory dot indicator underneath.
- **Center Add Action:** A punch-out or overlapping Candy Pink (`#F5ACD7`) circle (`54px × 54px`) harboring a bold `#171717` plus sign (`+`).

### 2. Bento Cards
- **Structure:** Corner radius of `24px` to `28px`, filled with pastel tokens (`#F5ACD7`, `#F9DF72`, `#B7C5F3`, `#B8E98E`, `#A5E8EF`, or `#E7A2EE`).
- **Content Rhythm:** Top left houses category or status badges; center features punchy Space Grotesk statistics or bold task names; bottom contains small metadata labels and interactive toggle pills.
- **Nested Lists:** When cards present sub-items (e.g., training drills or medical visits), each item rests in a high-contrast pill shape (`#FFFFFF` with `70%` opacity or solid white).

### 3. Buttons & Action Chips
- **Primary Button:** `#171717` solid pill with `#FAF6EC` text. Height `52px`, `label-lg` font.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with `#171717` text and optional `1.5px` border or soft pastel tint fill.
- **Quick Action Chips:** Pill-shaped (`36px` height) with soft ivory or pastel backgrounds, paired with leading micro-icons (e.g., pill dosage, water glass, heart icon).

### 4. Checkboxes & Habit Toggles
- **Habit Check:** Circular toggle (`28px × 28px`). Unchecked state is a clean neutral outline (`1.5px` border in `#171717` or `#6E6D68`). Checked state fills with Butter Yellow (`#F9DF72`) or Matcha Green (`#B8E98E`) with a crisp black checkmark (`✓`).

### 5. Input Fields & Steppers
- **Fields:** Rounded pill or `16px` squircle containers in `#FFFFFF`, with `14px` Plus Jakarta Sans typography.
- **Incremental Stepper:** Split pill with circular `-` and `+` buttons flanking a centered bold numeric display.

### 6. Organic Starburst Badges & Habit Stickers
- Compact vector badges (`32px` to `48px`) styled with playful rotation. Used inside hero widgets to represent overall wellness scores (e.g., sparkling flower or starburst next to score `8.8`).
